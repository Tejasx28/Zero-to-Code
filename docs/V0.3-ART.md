# Original campaign artwork

The campaign hero was generated with the built-in image generation tool, then inspected and copied into the project as `public/art/command-academy.png`. No CLI/API fallback was used.

The art provides an original tactical-anime setting inspired by the requested strategic atmosphere: an angular masked strategist, a forest-green night, sand-beige fortress and chess silhouettes, and a restrained electric-purple focal point. It contains no written text, interface, franchise logo, watermark, weapons, or copied franchise character.

The strategist occupies the right third. The left half is mostly deep-green sky and atmosphere for readable campaign text. Sand architecture supplies depth; purple remains a small accent around the eye and energy arcs. Use cover sizing and a center-right position, with a subtle forest-green gradient behind text. On narrow screens, separate long copy from the portrait rather than covering the face.

The original story framing is **The Cipher Rebellion**: a learner joins a command academy to restore a city’s lost knowledge through plans and code. The strategist’s role is to ask useful questions, not issue complete solutions. The independent task can be understood as an unwritten order: the learner receives a problem and tests, then decides how to solve it.
